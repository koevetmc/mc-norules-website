The Arena

Void-world pit. Right-click ArenaMaster at the south spawn gate (not from the wild). Gear is vaulted, diamond kit on entry, /arena leave restores it at spawn. PvE waves, named bosses, 1v1 or a bot. Punch a boss head in the lobby to spar. Party of 3 max.

🎤

Proximity voice

Simple Voice Chat on the server. You need the matching client mod (Fabric / NeoForge / Forge / Quilt). Vanilla and Bedrock have no voice — that is how SVC works. See install notes.

⚖

Anti-cheat, not anti-fun

GrimAC + packetevents. Cheating is the only bannable offense. Anti-xray engine-mode 2 is on.

Hall of Legends

In-game statue hall at spawn for the #1 player in 24 vanilla stat categories, plus Server Firsts (/firsts) for first-on-this-map records. This site mirrors the legends boards. Open the hall.

Anarchy resource pack

Optional UI pack. No popup on join. In game: /pack menu or /pack low|medium|high|off — chunky iron / worn iron + oxblood / extra rust, rivets, ember trim, or vanilla UI.

VGS quick-chat

Tribes-style voice-game-soundboard. Press ; for a sidebar of 70+ tactical callouts, or fire a code like ;af. F-key hand swap is vanilla (no dupe). Plays a sound to everyone.

※

Party ping + share

Middle-click a block or player to ping that spot for your /party (or /ping). Marker on the locator bar. /share still broadcasts a held item. Not in a party = invite someone first.

◉

Locator bar + /wp

Party members see each other on the vanilla locator bar. /wp waypoints: private by default, share with a player or party, HUD roster with distance. Outsiders see nothing.

→

/autofollow

/autofollow <player> (alias /follow) follows someone within 5 blocks. Sneak, damage, or /autofollow off cancels. The jar is staged on the server — it loads at the next restart, not before.

ChatFX
